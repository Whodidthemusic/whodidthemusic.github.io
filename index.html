CRT Sample

Modern crate digging. Built for your DAW.

Crate digging has moved beyond the record store. For today's producers, YouTube and Instagram are part of the search. CRT Sample connects that discovery to production, turning the recordings you find into material you can chop and play.

Copy and paste a YouTube or Instagram link, or load audio and video from your computer or external drives. Find the best moments and chop them directly inside your DAW.

Play 32 monophonic audio/video chops using MIDI, your computer keyboard or the hardware-style pads, with playback locked to your session's tempo.

  • Paste YouTube + Instagram links
  • Load local + external drive media
  • Chop directly inside your DAW
  • Automatic BPM + key detection
  • DAW tempo lock
  • 32 monophonic chops
  • Waveform zoom + marker editing
  • Chorus, lo-fi, bitcrush + reverb
